# Product

Source: written from the client brief supplied in the first session (2026-10-09). The user asked not to be interviewed, so every fact below comes from that brief; nothing was added.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Brief-pinned: Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS, react-hook-form + zod, Motion, lucide-react. Hosted on Cloudflare Workers (free plan) through the OpenNext adapter. Code on GitHub: thomasdevving/newera-website.

## Users

Professional buyers of kitchens in Dutch residential construction: building contractors (aannemers), project developers, housing associations (woningcorporaties), installers and building partners. Typical scene: a contractor building a block of around 20 homes who needs 20 kitchens designed, quoted, ordered, measured, installed and handed over on schedule. Private individuals are not customers and are politely redirected.

## Product Purpose

Studio NewEra designs, supplies and installs kitchens exclusively for business customers. The public website builds trust with professional buyers, explains the process, converts visitors into structured project requests, and gives existing customers an obvious login entry point. Success is a submitted, complete request (with floor plans) instead of an inconsistent email.

## Positioning

One organised partner for the whole chain: aanvraag, uitwerking, offerte, akkoord, meten, bestelling, montage, oplevering, service. One contact person, overview in every phase, delays made visible early.

## Operating Context

Requests usually arrive as floor plans (PDF/photo/DWG) by email. Buyers work with delivery weeks ("week 40 2026"), planning schedules, multiple identical units per project. Known pain points: inconsistent request formats, scattered communication, no status visibility, late discovery of delays (for example kitchens not yet measured when the delivery week approaches).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current: design, technical drawing, piping and electrical advice, quoting, measuring on site, ordering, installation, handover, after-sales.
- Future (not to be presented as existing): customer portal with requests, status per phase, quotes and drawings, automatic notifications; AI-assisted processing; 3D rendering.
- The portal route exists only as a placeholder; no authentication, no database, no payments.
- No CAD/ERP integration claims, no B2C shop, pricing or catalogue.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Studio NewEra. Logo supplied by the user as an image: "STUDIO" small and widely spaced above a large geometric "NewEra" in off-white, the E drawn as three gold bars, a thin gold arc sweeping over the top right. Stored at `public/brand/logo.png`.
- Visible copy in Dutch (nl-NL); tone professional, direct, calm, confident. No hype, no exclamation marks.
- User's own direction for the site: as tight ("strak") as possible, few soft edges, medium to heavy animation, no em-dashes in copy.
- Second round (client review, 2026-10-12): much shorter pages, subtle motion, photo-led look following a reference image the user supplied (full-bleed kitchen photography, one warm light band, uppercase navigation, slide counter). Keep Outfit and the logo's gold arc and "E" bars instead of copying the reference's serif.
- Small uppercase section labels with a short rule above headings are wanted: the brief names the hero label "KEUKENS VOOR PROFESSIONALS" and the reference uses the same device.

## Evidence on Hand

Only the logo. No client names, testimonials, statistics, certifications, awards, founding year, address, KvK number, phone or email. These must remain labelled placeholders until supplied.

## Product Principles

1. Overview over persuasion: show how a project stays on schedule rather than claiming it.
2. Business only: every route speaks to professional buyers.
3. Honest about the roadmap: future portal features are labelled as future.
4. One place for everything: a request, a project, a contact person.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigable, visible focus, linked form errors, respects prefers-reduced-motion.
